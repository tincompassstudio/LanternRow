Privacy Policy — Lantern Row

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Tin Compass Studio (“we”, “us”) develops Lantern Row (the “Game”). This policy explains what data the Game and the third-party services inside it collect, and what we do and do not have access to. It describes the Game as currently published.

Summary

We run no server and receive no personal data about you. Your puzzles are generated on your own device and your progress stays there. The Game does include two Google services — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — and those services collect data of their own, described below. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally, using your platform's standard app storage. We never receive it and cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it, unless your device's own backup to your Google account is turned on, in which case Android may restore it when you reinstall — that backup is Google's, encrypted and under your control, and never reaches us:

  • Your daily-puzzle streak, best streak, and the date you last solved one
  • How long tonight's puzzle took you, and how many hints you used on it, so the result can be shared again later
  • Which levels you have unlocked, and which you have finished at least once
  • Which nights you have solved, with your time and hints on each, for the past-nights list and your stats
  • How many boards of each level you have solved, and your best time on each
  • The board you are partway through, so you can pick it up where you left off
  • How many lanterns Lantern Street last showed you, so new ones light up once
  • Hint credits, and how many free and ad-earned hints you have used today
  • Settings: theme choice, music, sound, piece names, mistake outlining, vibration, clear confirmation
  • Whether a supporter purchase has been recorded

There is no account and no sign-in, so none of this is tied to an identity.

How the puzzles work

Every board is generated on your device from the date, using a fixed algorithm. That is why everyone gets the same daily puzzle without us running a server, and why the Game plays normally with no network connection. Nothing about your solutions, your times, or your progress is transmitted anywhere.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows two kinds of ad:

  • Rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a hint. These are never forced and never interrupt a puzzle.
  • Occasional full-screen ads, shown only after you finish a practice board — never during a puzzle, and never on the daily puzzle.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data directly — we never see it. Depending on your settings and region, AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse region)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, clicked)

This is used to deliver and measure ads and, unless you opt out, to personalise them. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Your consent choice

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or another region where consent is required, the Game shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and a Settings → Ad privacy choices entry appears inside the Game so you can change that choice later. Where consent is not required, that entry is not shown. If consent cannot be obtained, the Game simply does not show ads — it remains fully playable.

You can also limit ad tracking at the device level in Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

The Game offers optional one-time purchases: hint packs, and a “Supporter” purchase that removes the full-screen ads. Nothing in the Game is locked behind a purchase.

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details — we do not see your card number, billing address, or name. The Game receives only a confirmation from Google that a specific product was bought, which it uses to grant the item. Purchase history and payment data are handled by Google under their own privacy policy.

Your purchases are tied to your Google account, not to us, which is why they come back when you reinstall the Game or sign in on a new device.

What we do not collect

We operate no server and no account system. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or files. The Game contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no social features and no chat.

The Game itself asks only for internet access and network state, so that ads can load and purchases can be confirmed with Google Play. The Google ads and billing libraries add the permissions they need to work — access to the advertising ID, Play Billing, and short background tasks. None of them asks for your approval, and none gives access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children's privacy

Lantern Row is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no mature content. Because the Game includes advertising and in-app purchases, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise its use by minors. Google Play offers purchase authentication controls for this.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the advertising service, contact us and we will assist you in raising it with Google.
